What to record in your own shortlist
Field What to verify
Listing & variant Save the source link, seller name, selected color and exact variant. Similar images may belong to different listings.
Sizing Record measurements and units. Compare them with a well-fitting item you already own.
Materials Note the stated composition and care guidance. Ask about missing or ambiguous details.
Photos & condition Review available item photos for visible construction, color, included accessories and obvious defects.
Costs Separate the item price from applicable delivery, service, conversion and destination charges.
Terms & date Check current availability, return conditions and the date you last reviewed the listing.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize links and comparison notes for shopping research. The information included varies by its creator. Verify each original listing before relying on prices, sizes or availability.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is an independent shopping guide and category directory. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, where you can browse product listings.

How should I compare shoe and clothing sizes?

For shoes, compare the listing’s size chart and stated foot or insole measurements. For clothing, compare chest, shoulder, length and waist measurements with an item you already own. Do not assume that a familiar size label guarantees the same fit.

What should I check in QC photos?

Where quality-control photos are available, check the selected color, visible construction, measurements, accessories and obvious defects. Photos alone cannot establish authenticity, long-term durability or exact fit.

Is the listed product price the final cost?

Not necessarily. Depending on the seller, service and destination, additional costs may include domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion and applicable taxes or duties.

Is this an official Oopbuy or designer-brand website?

No. This is an independent guide directing visitors to CAFANS. Brand names are used as editorial references. The illustrations are conceptual and do not represent verified products, stock or brand endorsements.
